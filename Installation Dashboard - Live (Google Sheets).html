📂 Local File
Company OneDrive (Microsoft 365) — Paste your OneDrive sharing link and the dashboard will attempt a direct connection. If your organization requires sign-in, use the Local File tab instead (drag from your synced OneDrive folder).

Get your OneDrive sharing link

  1. Open your Excel file in OneDrive or SharePoint
  2. Click Share → Copy link
  3. Set sharing to "Anyone with the link" or "People in [Company]"
  4. Paste the link below — the dashboard will try to download it automatically
Supports SharePoint, OneDrive for Business, and personal OneDrive links
📁 Drag your Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file here — or click to browse
Works great if OneDrive syncs to your PC (look in your OneDrive folder)

Connect your Google Sheet (2 steps)

  1. Open your Google Sheet → click Share (top-right) → change access to "Anyone with the link" → set role to Viewer
  2. Click Copy link and paste it below
💡 First time? Go to sheets.google.com, create a new sheet, then File → Import → Upload the Installation Data for Google Sheets.csv file included in your folder.
Paste the link from Share → Copy link — any Google Sheets URL format is accepted
Load any .xlsx or .csv file from your computer. If OneDrive syncs to your PC, just open your OneDrive folder and drag the file here.
